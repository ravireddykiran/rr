Stream won't play here? Read this.

Browsers are the most restrictive way to watch IPTV. A stream can be perfectly fine and still refuse to play in a web page because the broadcaster's server doesn't send CORS headers, because it is plain http:// on an https:// page, or because it is geo-blocked to its home country. Use Copy URL or Open in VLC and it will usually work in VLC, TiviMate, Kodi or any TV app. See docs/APPS.md in the repository for free apps on every device.
