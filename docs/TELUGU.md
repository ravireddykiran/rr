# Telugu on your phone — what's actually free

Short version: **free-to-air Telugu news, Doordarshan and devotional channels stream free and legally. The big entertainment and movie channels (Star Maa, Zee Telugu, Gemini, ETV, Star Maa Movies, ETV Cinema, Tolly TV) are pay-TV and have no stable free legal stream.** That blue "content partner has blocked this content in your region" screen is a pay-TV channel's unofficial re-stream being blocked. It is not something a different link fixes reliably.

## If a free channel doesn't open

- These are India-region streams. They work best from an Indian connection; from abroad most are geo-blocked.
- In your app, long-press the channel and switch source — most have 2 to 20 backups.
- If a scraped link is dead, open that channel's **official YouTube live** instead (search "<channel name> live", e.g. "TV9 Telugu live"). Every major Telugu news channel runs an official 24/7 YouTube live stream. In OTT Navigator / TiviMate you can add a YouTube live URL as a channel; in the plain YouTube app it just plays.
